# Reach — first public preview

Reach is free, MIT-licensed browser communication software with hand/finger movement, a visible pointer and large selectable phrases. This public preview emphasizes hand setup while retaining touch, switch scanning and experimental webcam eye control.

- Prominent hand-control entry, explicit camera start and permission-failure recovery.
- Personalizable care/family phrase boards in six languages.
- More choices on larger screens and fewer on phones; selectable page/scroll controls and rest.
- Local camera processing, no account or AI subscription required.
- Editable HyperFrames introduction, narration, subtitles and source-backed cost comparison.

Download `reach-hand-communication.mp4` to watch the 2:11 introduction. `reach-captions.srt` supplies separate subtitles, and `film-contact-sheet.jpg` previews the six scenes. The editable video is in `videos/reach-hands/`.

Software is free; a compatible device, webcam and any mounting or assistance are separate costs. Reach controls its own interface, not the whole operating system. Camera input needs testing with the intended person and setup; eye mode is experimental. The film labels its pointer animation illustrative and compares distinct software/hardware packages without claiming equal performance.

Build and 78 unit tests passed, along with focused simulated-input/browser checks. The rendered MP4 passed full decode and browser playback verification. See the README, CONTRIBUTING.md, video SOURCES.md and VERIFICATION.md for details.

This release publishes source and the video. It is not a separately hosted website; see README.md for running or hosting the app.
