## Demonstration boundaries

Screen captures come from a clean browser profile with sample phrases. The animated pointer on the actual app screenshot illustrates the interaction; it does not record a real hand or establish tracking accuracy. The app has automated unit/browser coverage, not clinical validation. Real-device testing with intended users remains necessary. Eye tracking is explicitly experimental.

The narration's view of constructive AI use is our editorial position: build useful tools, reduce barriers, make the code inspectable, keep camera tracking local, and preserve the person's choice of words. It does not claim AI can infer consent or replace care.
