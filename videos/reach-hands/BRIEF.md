## Intent
The user requested a finished HyperFrames video explaining Reach, focusing on hand movement, comparing hand and eye tracking setups and costs with free Reach software, and showing a constructive use of AI. The user explicitly chose a public GitHub repository.

## Execution decisions
Produce the finished video autonomously under the user's request to make and deliver it. Use English narration, readable captions, about two minutes, landscape 1080p. Narration uses the available local Kokoro engine. No music is needed under this accessibility-focused explanation. Show real captured app screens; any animated pointer demonstration is labeled illustrative and is not evidence of clinical or physical-camera validation.

## Assets
Local Reach preview at http://localhost:4173, source code, actual browser screenshots, authored diagrams and factual comparison cards. No patient images, private profiles, testimonials or invented human trials.

## Cost rules
Reach's software costs zero; an existing compatible device and webcam are required. Do not represent hardware or support as free. Name other free options fairly. Price labels identify currency, product scope, date, taxes and exclusions. Dedicated systems are not equivalent in capability or support. Eye mode remains experimental.

## Deliverables
Public repository, finished narrated MP4, captions/transcript, source ledger, editable HyperFrames project, and verification evidence.
